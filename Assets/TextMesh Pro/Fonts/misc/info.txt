Antarcdika
Desgined by Heaven Castro

-About the font-
Antarcdika is a shattered sans serif font based on Andika by SIL International and was inspired by Land of the Lustrous character, Antarcticite. During episode 8 of the anime adaptation produced by Orange Co, Ltd., Antarcticite was badly damaged while trying to fight Lunarians by attempting to grab her sword and her hand back but was later shattered by them and took her away to the moon, leaving Phosphophyllite as a successor to her.

-Font naming information-
The font's name is a combination of the African word "andika" and a rare, freezing solid mineral called antarcticite, named after a continent of Antarctica, where this place is where this mineral was first discovered.

-Is this font part of something?-
This font is part of Doujin Fonts series.

-The typographical inspiration-
This font was only made in tribute to Dutch animator, digital artist, graphic designer and former font maker Sander Kessels. Check out his webstie at lollibomb.com (in Dutch).

-Font use reccomendation-
Due to the shattering and cracked effects of the font (although this one can function both as a text and display font), you must use this font at slightly larger sizes to see such details more clearly. Also, don't use the bold and italic styles as it only has one style and weight.
